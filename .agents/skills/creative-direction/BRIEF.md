# Experience brief outline

Adapt this outline into the project's existing design document, or `DESIGN.md`. Keep only useful sections, answer with specific decisions, and link existing material instead of duplicating it. This is a working artifact, not a sequence of approval gates.

## Audience and outcome

- Audience, context of use, and core task.
- Main action and a short useful journey, including repeated use where relevant.
- Intended feeling and what a visitor should remember, when relevant. A clear, reliable workflow can be the aim for a utility. Label emotional and recall claims as hypotheses until researched.
- Scope, constraints, user decisions already made, and remaining consequential questions.

## Direction

- Selected expression: restrained, expressive, or immersive. Explain its fit to the task.
- Organizing idea grounded in this product's domain, content, and identity.
- References studied: URL, date, observed surface or behavior, useful principle, and observation limits.
- Concepts compared, if needed, and why the chosen direction won. Link prototypes and evidence.

## Visual and content system

- Palette roles, typography, hierarchy, composition, density, and mobile treatment.
- Voice, domain vocabulary, and representative real content.
- Asset plan: role, source or creation method, rights or license status, format, responsive treatment, and fallback. Mark placeholders and unresolved dependencies.

## Interaction and states

- Opening, main action, feedback, and next step; narrative scenes only when appropriate.
- Important triggers, input methods, timing, settled states, interruption, reset, and recovery.
- Loading, empty, error, success, and return states that the journey needs.
- Keyboard, touch, reduced-motion, and optional-media fallbacks.

## Acceptance and evidence

| Intended outcome | Check and conditions | Evidence or remaining gap |
|---|---|---|

Cover the core task, visual identity, important interactions, accessibility, and measured performance targets. Keep design judgments and user-research hypotheses distinct from verified behavior. Include device, browser, viewport, and network conditions when they affect a result.

## Implementation handoff

- Decisions and acceptance checks implementation must preserve.
- Dependencies and unresolved limits that could change the outcome.
- Links to the running artifact, captures, measurements, and subsequent decisions.
